# Technical writing, translation and office deliverables

## Languages and formats

Technical documents in English and Chinese for the EGC package and other compressor scopes (such as a Habshan
project package), bilingual reports, and translated source records, delivered as Word,
PDF, Excel and PowerPoint.

## Translating a technical handbook

A long technical handbook translated in sequence, chapter by chapter, then the appendix and index, finishing each
chapter in one part where possible and never losing track of where the work had reached.

## Quality and correspondence

Rewriting quality requirements, minutes of meeting, material acceptance wording, contractual scope questions, issue
trackers and pre-contract open-item letters, in professional wording that keeps the technical meaning exact.

## Templates and presentations

Simple Word CV templates with a photo box; progress-meeting presentations corrected for mistakes and poor tables.
Deliverables have to be real files with working downloads: saying that a file exists is not enough.
