# Export Gas Compressor package (API 617), ADNOC Sahil Phase 3

Supply and engineering coordination for the Export Gas Compressor (EGC) package of the ADNOC Sahil Phase 3
Development Project: an electric-motor-driven, gear-driven centrifugal compressor package built to API 617.
TARGET Engineering is the EPC contractor; SBW (Shengu) is the compressor manufacturer and package supplier.
The work covers technical clarifications, design review, compliance verification against ADNOC specifications
(AGES) and the purchase specification, progress tracking, sub-vendor management, manufacturing record books and
factory acceptance testing.

## Sub-vendors and their scope

- ABB: main induction motor (Finland), variable frequency drive (Switzerland) and 33 kV gas-insulated switchgear.
- Hitachi: VFD transformer.
- Rittal: VFD chiller (cooling for the drive).
- EagleBurgmann: dry gas seals (Germany) and the seal gas booster and seal gas console.
- Flender: gearbox between the motor and the compressor.
- Kelvion: lube oil air coolers (air-cooled heat exchangers).
- KOSO: anti-surge valve.
- CCC (Compressor Controls Corporation): load sharing, anti-surge and performance control.
- Titania Aero: proximity probes for shaft vibration and axial position.
- Siemens: pressure transmitters and other instrumentation (SITRANS P range).

## The equipment in the package

Compressor; dry gas seals; gearbox; bearings; couplings; seal gas console and booster; lube oil system and its air
cooler; anti-surge valve; main motor; VFD with its transformer and chiller; gas-insulated switchgear and isolators;
and the unit control panel. Each item comes from a different sub-vendor, so most of the work is making their
interfaces meet.

## Interfaces between sub-vendors

The driver train (ABB motor, VFD, Hitachi transformer, switchgear) has to agree with the Flender gearbox ratio, the
couplings and the compressor's speed range. EagleBurgmann's dry gas seals need a seal gas supply and booster sized
for start-up, settle-out and pressurised standstill. The lube oil system serves the bearings, gearbox and couplings
and needs Kelvion coolers sized for the site's ambient temperature. The KOSO anti-surge valve and the CCC controller
have to match the compressor map and the recycle loop. Titania Aero probes and their monitors follow API 670. All of
it has to fit one baseframe and one control philosophy.

## Typical technical questions

- Valve fugitive-emission testing: lot sampling under MESC SPE 77/312 and ISO 15848 for anti-surge valves of
  different sizes and stem diameters, and whether one sample can represent a mixed lot.
- Noise during the mechanical running test above the datasheet guarantee: the test arrangement (discharge to
  atmosphere through a long duct), measurement conditions, A-weighting and background correction all matter before
  calling it a nonconformance.
- A replacement compressor on an existing baseframe: interface dimensions, alignment and foundation loads.
- Shipping a complete heavy skid (compressor, gearbox, motor and baseframe): heavy-lift or break-bulk transport.
- CCC control scope: whether master load sharing, anti-surge control and choke protection sit in one controller or
  separate ones, and whether configuring choke alarms and trips falls within a modification scope.
- Material acceptance: when ADNOC project standards and a manufacturer's derivative of a public material standard
  (for example of ASTM B637 for alloy 718) both apply, the deviation and the evidence of equivalence must be stated.
- Weld leaks found at hydrotest (for example where a drain pipe joins a cast stainless pump casing): root cause,
  corrective action and a qualified repair before any retest.
- A further propane compressor scope: a separate schedule, unrelated scope removed, exchanged with the scheduler.

## Quality submissions for a multi-compressor package

A project quality plan for the whole package; an inspection and test plan per compressor type; a procurement and
manufacturing status register to the EPC; a weld book with WPS, PQR, overlay and consumable procedures (ASME IX);
one NDE procedure with an NDE map; one PMI procedure; casing hydrotest procedures; assembled-compressor leak tests
after the mechanical running test; and an MRB index per package.
