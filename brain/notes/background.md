# Background

Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering. Bachelor's thesis: deformation behaviour and impact energy
absorption of metal tubes under axial compression. Papers on strain-hardening effects during plastic buckling of
axially compressed aluminium tubes, and elementary experimental studies on stress wave propagation in bars (2019,
with P. B. Shahapur and T. Y. Reddy).

Master's degree in Advanced Engineering Materials. Master's thesis: effect of prior deformation on slip activity in
nickel alloys, measuring slip, grain orientation and microstructure with high-resolution digital image correlation
(HRDIC) and EBSD maps.

Tools used in research: MATLAB, Python (NumPy, SciPy, pandas, Matplotlib), Abaqus CAE, SolidWorks, Fusion 360,
Arduino and embedded C, strain gauges, instrumentation amplifiers and oscilloscopes.
